# Code of Conduct / Código de conducta

## English
This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
by reference. In short: be respectful, welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels,
give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable. Maintainers may remove contributions and restrict participation to enforce this.

Report unacceptable behavior to **design@albertobalbuena.com**. Reports are handled privately and
in good faith; enforcement follows the guidelines in the Covenant.

## Español
Este proyecto adopta por referencia el
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/es/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
respeto, acogida a personas de cualquier origen y nivel, crítica constructiva y foco en el bien de la
comunidad. No se toleran el acoso, los ataques personales, la discriminación ni publicar datos
privados de otras personas. Reporta conductas inaceptables a **design@albertobalbuena.com**.
